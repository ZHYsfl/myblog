AI-Whys now includes notes and code implementations for BERT, Codec, and GAN, bringing the collection to 40 experiments!

Figure 1 · BERT: learning language through fill-in-the-blank training. The model randomly masks 15% of the words in a sentence and learns to recover them, while also predicting whether two sentences form a continuous context. After pretraining once, BERT can be fine-tuned for classification, question answering, named entity recognition, and more. BERT laid the foundation for NLP.

![BERT pretraining and downstream fine-tuning architecture](/assets/posts/ai-whys-bert-codec-gan/093a07cc061da97e420771e4ee6090d0.jpg)

Figure 2 · Neural Audio Codec (including GAN-based training): a foundation for large speech models. Large language models need text tokens, and large speech models likewise need speech tokens. How are those speech tokens obtained? That is exactly what a Codec does. Its training methods include GANs (generative adversarial networks).

![Neural Audio Codec and GAN training architecture](/assets/posts/ai-whys-bert-codec-gan/42dcecb9b0c330aaa04441c264a8d66e.jpg)
